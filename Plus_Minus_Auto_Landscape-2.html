How to play

  • Play against three computer opponents in a four-player match.
  • Each round starts with five cards each. Players choose a trump suit, then everyone receives 13 cards.
  • Bid how many tricks you think you can win. The trump selector's minimum bid follows their trump selection bid.
  • Follow the led suit if you can. If you cannot, play trump when available; overtrump when required by the rules.
  • Meet or beat your trick bid to gain points equal to your bid. Miss it and lose points equal to your bid.
  • The player with the lowest total score deals the next round. Reach the match target to finish.
  • Match rewards: 1st place earns 10 💎, 2nd earns 5 💎, 3rd earns 2 💎. Designs cost 50 💎 each.
